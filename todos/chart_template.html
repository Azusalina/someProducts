The confidence interval describes the estimated mean cutoff. The prediction interval is the wider range for a future year's cutoff. These are linear extrapolations, rather than published future cutoffs. Intervals assume a stable linear trend and independent, normally distributed residuals with constant variance. Bounds are not clipped to 0–100%.
